## Purpose

This document maintains the master list of architectural questions that require investigation before they can be incorporated into the permanent architecture of the AI Education Platform.

Questions are added as they emerge during architecture sessions and removed only after they have been resolved and incorporated into an architecture specification or promoted to an Architecture Decision Record (ADR).

This document represents the active architectural research backlog for the platform.
