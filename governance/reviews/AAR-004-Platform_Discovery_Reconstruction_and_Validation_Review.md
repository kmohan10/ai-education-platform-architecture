# AAR-004 – Platform Discovery Reconstruction & Validation Review

## Background

You are joining this project as an independent Chief Architect.

You have **no prior knowledge** of this repository and no access to the conversations that produced it.

Treat this engagement exactly as if you had been asked by an institution to evaluate an existing constitutional architecture repository created by another architecture team.

The attached ZIP file contains the complete repository.

The repository claims to contain:

- a validated Institutional Knowledge Architecture Methodology,
- a completed Institutional Knowledge Repository,
- and the completed constitutional discovery of an Institutional Intelligence Platform.

Your task is **not** to critique individual documents.

Instead, use the repository to understand the constitutional methodology and then evaluate whether the completed **Platform Discovery** artifact successfully communicates the constitutional definition of the Institutional Intelligence Platform.

Do not assume the repository is correct.

Every conclusion should be independently derived from the repository itself.

---

# Primary Objective

Determine whether the completed **Platform Discovery** artifact functions as a complete and authoritative constitutional artifact.

Specifically determine whether an experienced architect, unfamiliar with the repository, can reconstruct the Institutional Intelligence Platform from the Platform Discovery package without requiring knowledge of the conversations that produced it.

The purpose of this review is to validate the quality of the constitutional artifact itself rather than the conversations that created it.

---

# Review Scope

The review should follow the same progression that a new architect would naturally follow.

## Phase 1 — Repository Orientation

Read the repository onboarding material to understand the constitutional methodology.

This includes:

- README.md
- START_HERE.md
- docs/foundations/

The purpose of this phase is only to understand:

- repository philosophy,
- constitutional methodology,
- governance,
- architectural principles,
- terminology.

Do not attempt to reconstruct the Platform during this phase.

---

## Phase 2 — Platform Discovery Review

Read the Platform Discovery package.

Treat this package as the primary subject of the review.

Assume no additional Platform artifacts exist.

Do not rely upon future Platform Vision, Platform Architecture, or Platform Positioning documents.

Evaluate Platform Discovery entirely on its own merits.

---

## Phase 3 — Independent Platform Reconstruction

Using only the Platform Discovery artifact and the constitutional context established during repository orientation, independently reconstruct the Platform.

Your reconstruction should identify:

- Institutional Intelligence
- Platform Identity
- Platform Mission
- Constitutional Responsibilities
- Constitutional Boundaries
- Behavioural Integrity
- Constitutional Operationalization
- Repository Evolution vs Platform Learning
- Platform Stakeholders
- Platform Governance
- Relationship to the Repository
- Relationship to Domain Intelligence Platforms
- Relationship to Domain Products

Do not attempt to reproduce the wording of the document.

Instead, reconstruct your own understanding from the architectural evidence presented.

---

## Phase 4 — Architectural Validation

Evaluate whether Platform Discovery:

- communicates a coherent constitutional architecture,
- establishes clear constitutional responsibilities,
- maintains correct authority partitioning,
- distinguishes Repository responsibilities from Platform responsibilities,
- consistently defines Platform boundaries,
- naturally leads into Platform Specification,
- provides sufficient constitutional guidance for downstream Domain Intelligence Platforms.

Identify any:

- architectural strengths,
- ambiguities,
- inconsistencies,
- missing concepts,
- unnecessary complexity,
- opportunities for clarification.

Support every observation with evidence from the repository.

---

## Phase 5 — Platform Specification Readiness

Determine whether the repository is ready to proceed with:

- Platform Vision
- Platform Architecture
- Platform Positioning

If additional constitutional discovery is required, identify exactly what remains.

If the repository is ready, explain why.
