The review identified a stable set of architectural invariants that consistently explain the repository's constitutional structure.

These include:

- One authoritative source for every constitutional concern.
- One constitutional responsibility per architectural artifact.
- Constitutional authority flows in one direction.
- Authority precedes dependency.
- Governance enables evolution.
- Meaning is preserved.
- Canonical representation is governed.
- Institutional Knowledge is constructed upon Canonical Representation.
- Knowledge preserves.
- Reasoning derives.
- Reasoning cannot redefine institutional knowledge.
- Implementation cannot redefine reasoning.

These invariants emerged independently during reconstruction and remained consistent throughout peer review and validation.
