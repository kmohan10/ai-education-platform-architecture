# Platform Specifications

## Overview

This folder contains the authoritative constitutional specifications of the **Institutional Intelligence Platform**.

Platform Discovery establishes and validates the constitutional understanding of the Institutional Intelligence Platform.

Platform Specification codifies those validated discoveries into authoritative constitutional specifications.

As Platform Discovery matures, additional specifications emerge incrementally rather than waiting for discovery to conclude.

Together, these specifications establish the enduring constitutional definition of the Institutional Intelligence Platform and provide the authoritative architectural foundation for all downstream Domain Intelligence Platforms.

---

# Relationship to Platform Discovery

The documents in this folder are **derived from** the constitutional discoveries contained in `docs/platform_discovery`.

Platform Discovery is responsible for:

- discovering constitutional principles;
- validating architectural hypotheses;
- reducing concepts to their fundamental constitutional form; and
- establishing the constitutional understanding of the Platform.

Platform Specification is responsible for:

- codifying validated constitutional discoveries;
- establishing authoritative architectural specifications;
- preserving the constitutional definition of the Platform; and
- providing the constitutional foundation for all downstream Platform realizations.

Discovery precedes Specification at the artifact level.

As constitutional discoveries mature and become sufficiently validated, they are codified into authoritative Platform Specifications while Platform Discovery continues to investigate the remaining constitutional questions.

Discovery and Specification therefore progress incrementally rather than as completely sequential phases.

---

# Constitutional Scope

This folder defines the constitutional specification of the Institutional Intelligence Platform.

It defines:

- the Platform's constitutional purpose;
- the Platform's constitutional organization;
- the Platform's constitutional responsibilities;
- the Platform's constitutional capabilities;
- the Platform's constitutional boundaries; and
- the Platform's constitutional position within the Institutional Knowledge Architecture.

It does **not** define:

- domain-specific intelligence platforms;
- domain products;
- implementation architecture;
- software design;
- technology selection;
- deployment architecture; or
- operational infrastructure.

Those concerns belong to downstream constitutional artifacts.

# Architectural Principles

The Platform Specifications collectively define the constitutional architecture of the Institutional Intelligence Platform.

Each specification owns a distinct constitutional responsibility.

Collectively they define a single constitutional architecture.

The Platform Specifications prescribe architectural principles rather than implementation technologies.

They do **not** prescribe:

- software frameworks;
- programming languages;
- deployment models;
- infrastructure technologies; or
- implementation patterns.

Implementation realizes the constitutional architecture.

It does not define it.

The Platform Specifications represent the authoritative constitutional definition of the Institutional Intelligence Platform.

They transform validated constitutional discoveries into enduring architectural specifications suitable for long-term institutional governance.
