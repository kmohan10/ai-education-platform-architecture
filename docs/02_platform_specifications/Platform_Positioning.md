The Institutional Intelligence Platform occupies the constitutional layer between the Institutional Knowledge Repository and Domain Intelligence Platforms within the Institutional Knowledge Architecture. It derives its constitutional authority from the Institutional Knowledge Methodology and consumes governed institutional knowledge and reasoning from the Repository. It is realized through Domain Intelligence Platforms, which in turn enable Domain Products that support Institutional Operations. Operational experience generated through these operations returns upstream through the established governance process to enable the governed evolution of institutional knowledge and reasoning. The constitutional purpose, responsibilities, capabilities, and governance of the Platform are specified in the Platform Vision and Platform Architecture.
