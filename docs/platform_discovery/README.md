# Platform Discovery

## Overview

This folder contains the constitutional discovery of the **Institutional Intelligence Platform**.

The purpose of this phase is not to design software or define implementation architecture.

Instead, it seeks to answer a more fundamental constitutional question:

> **What is the Institutional Intelligence Platform, and why must it exist?**

The discovery follows the constitutional methodology established by the Institutional Knowledge Methodology and the Institutional Knowledge Repository.

Rather than inventing a platform, the documents in this folder progressively discover, validate, and codify the constitutional responsibilities, boundaries, and purpose of the Platform.

---

# Discovery Objective

The primary objective of Platform Discovery is to establish the constitutional identity of the Institutional Intelligence Platform.

The discovery addresses questions such as:

- What is Institutional Intelligence?
- How does Institutional Intelligence differ from Institutional Knowledge and Institutional Reasoning?
- Why is the Institutional Knowledge Repository insufficient?
- What constitutional responsibility does the Platform fulfill?
- What capabilities belong to the Platform?
- What responsibilities remain outside the Platform?
- How does the Platform relate to Domain Intelligence Platforms and Domain Products?

The answers to these questions become the constitutional foundation for all subsequent Platform artifacts.

---

# Discovery Methodology

Platform Discovery follows the same constitutional methodology used throughout the repository:

```
Discover
      ↓
Validate
      ↓
Codify
```

Architectural concepts are not introduced as assumptions.

Instead, they emerge through systematic constitutional discovery and cross-domain validation before being formally codified.

---

# Expected Outcomes

Completion of Platform Discovery establishes:

- The constitutional definition of Institutional Intelligence.
- The constitutional identity of the Institutional Intelligence Platform.
- The Platform's mission and institutional purpose.
- Constitutional boundaries and responsibilities.
- Behavioural Integrity as the Platform's constitutional objective.
- Constitutional Operationalization as the mechanism through which Institutional Intelligence is realized.
- The relationship between the Repository, Platform, Domain Platforms, and Domain Products.

These discoveries provide the constitutional foundation for the next phase of the repository.
