# Platform Discovery

## Overview

This folder documents the constitutional discovery of the **Institutional Intelligence Platform** and establishes the constitutional foundation from which the Platform Specifications are derived.

The purpose of Platform Discovery is not to design software or define implementation architecture.

Instead, it seeks to answer the constitutional questions that establish the Platform's identity, purpose, responsibilities, boundaries, and architectural relationships.

> **What is the Institutional Intelligence Platform, why must it exist, and how should it be understood within the Institutional Knowledge Architecture?**

Platform Discovery follows the constitutional methodology established by the Institutional Knowledge Methodology and the Institutional Knowledge Repository.

Rather than inventing a platform, the documents in this folder progressively discover, validate, and codify the constitutional principles that define the Platform.

---

# Discovery Objective

The primary objective of Platform Discovery is to establish the constitutional identity of the Institutional Intelligence Platform.

The discovery addresses questions such as:

- What is Institutional Intelligence?
- How does Institutional Intelligence differ from Institutional Knowledge and Institutional Reasoning?
- Why is the Institutional Knowledge Repository insufficient?
- What constitutional responsibility does the Platform fulfill?
- What capabilities belong to the Platform?
- What responsibilities remain outside the Platform?
- Where does the Platform belong within the Institutional Knowledge Architecture?
- How does the Platform relate to adjacent architectural categories?
- How does the Platform relate to Domain Intelligence Platforms and Domain Products?

The validated answers to these questions become the constitutional foundation for all Platform Specifications.

---

# Discovery Methodology

Platform Discovery follows the same constitutional methodology used throughout the repository.

```text
Discover
      ↓
Validate
      ↓
Codify
      ↓
Specify
      ↓
Independently Validate
```

Architectural concepts are not introduced as assumptions.

They emerge through systematic constitutional discovery, cross-domain validation, and independent architectural review before being codified as authoritative repository knowledge.

Discovery precedes Specification **at the artifact level**.

As constitutional discoveries mature and become sufficiently validated, they are codified into authoritative Platform Specifications while Platform Discovery continues to investigate the remaining constitutional questions.

Discovery and Specification therefore progress incrementally rather than as completely sequential phases.
