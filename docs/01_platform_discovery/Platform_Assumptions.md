An assumption is something accepted as true before discovery begins. If discovery later proves or refines it, it should move out of Assumptions and into Discovery.

# Platform Assumptions

## Overview

The discovery of the **Institutional Intelligence Platform** is founded upon a small number of explicit assumptions regarding the nature of institutions, institutional knowledge, and institutional operations.

These assumptions are accepted as the starting premises for the discovery process. They are not conclusions reached through architectural analysis, nor are they constitutional principles governing the resulting architecture.

The purpose of this document is to make those foundational assumptions explicit, providing a transparent basis for the constitutional discoveries documented in the Platform Discovery.

---

# Assumption 1: Institutions Possess Institutional Knowledge

Institutions possess knowledge that extends beyond the knowledge of individual members.

This institutional knowledge represents the accumulated concepts, relationships, practices, policies, and operational understanding developed through the institution's existence.

The discovery assumes the existence of institutional knowledge without attempting to prove it.

---

# Assumption 2: Institutional Knowledge Can Be Represented

Institutional knowledge can be represented in a form that supports governance, reasoning, and operational application.

This assumption establishes the possibility of capturing institutional knowledge in a structured and governable manner, forming the foundation for the Institutional Knowledge Repository.

---

# Assumption 3: Institutional Reasoning Can Be Externalized

Institutional reasoning can be made explicit rather than remaining solely within the minds of institutional experts.

This assumption enables institutional reasoning to be represented, governed, evaluated, and consistently applied across institutional operations.

---

# Assumption 4: Institutions Perform Governed Operations

Institutions exist to perform operations in pursuit of defined organizational objectives.

These operations are conducted within an institutional context and provide the practical environment in which institutional knowledge and reasoning are applied.

---

# Assumption 5: Operational Experience Exists

Institutional operations generate operational experience that can be observed, evaluated, and retained.

This assumption establishes that institutional operations produce evidence which may subsequently contribute to institutional learning through appropriate governance processes.

---

# Assumption 6: Common Operational Concerns Exist Across Institutional Domains

Different institutional domains share common operational concerns that extend beyond domain-specific knowledge.

This assumption provides the basis for discovering a domain-independent Institutional Intelligence Platform capable of supporting multiple domain realizations while preserving constitutional consistency.

---

# Assumption 7: Institutional Intelligence Can Be Realized Within Domain Contexts

The capabilities of an Institutional Intelligence Platform can be specialized to serve individual institutional domains.

This assumption establishes the possibility of Domain Intelligence Platforms that inherit common institutional capabilities while incorporating domain-specific knowledge, reasoning, and operational requirements.

---

# Architectural Boundary

This document records only the assumptions accepted prior to constitutional discovery.

It does not define:

- the constitutional responsibilities of the Institutional Intelligence Platform;
- the architecture of the Platform;
- the constitutional principles governing the Platform;
- the discoveries arising from architectural analysis.

These are established by the Platform Discovery and the Platform Specifications.
